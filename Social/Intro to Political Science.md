# Intro to Political Science

## Power, People, Politics

### Pluralism vs Elitism

Pluralism refers to a political organization
where multiple groups contribute equally to a political process,
whereas elitism means that a political process is governed by a small group of people.

### Supranational Organizations

Supranational organizations surpass national boundaries.

Here are examples of supranational organizations:

* EU;
* Mercosur.

## Comparative Political Institutions

### Checks and Balances

Within an ideal state, the power is separated among:

1. Legislative branch.
2. Executive branch.
3. Judiciary branch.

### The UK and the USA

In the UK, the legislature (the majority) party chooses a Prime Minister.
Whereas, in the US, people vote for a president separately.

## Political Parties

### Majoritarian Voting System

Majoritarian system says that a party wins by a majority vote.

### Proportional Representation

PR system is usually found in ethnically divided regions.

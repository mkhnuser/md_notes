# Intro to Political Science

## Power, People, Politics

### Pluralism vs Elitism

Pluralism refers to a political organization
where multiple groups contribute equally to a political process,
whereas elitism means that a political process is governed by a small group of people.

### Supranational Organizations

Supranational organizations surpass national boundaries.

Here are examples of supranational organizations:

* EU;
* Mercosur.

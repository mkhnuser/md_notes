## Schopenhauer and Comte

Schopenhauer points out that egoism pertains to all humans.
He believes altruism serves as an antidote to egoism.

Comte believes that people should strive to become a better society as a whole.
He puts the society before an individual and argues for sociocracy.

## Mill

Mill bases his philosophy on the principle of utility, hence the name utilitarianism.
The question one should ask: why is it useful to do one thing and not to do another?
And so, what's the most useful principle that maximizes happiness?

## Nietzsche

Will to power is the main driver behind people's actions.

## Benedict & Ayer

Ayer sees ethics through the lens of culture.
Benedict argues for environmental determinism: a person is a product of culture.
Further, the world consists of many different cultures, and so there is no one ethics.

## Foot

Foot has revived virtue ethics by drawing connections between a person and nature.
Aristotle was of great influence for her.

# Monetary Theory

## Money: Private or Public?

### Monopoly

Once a coinage monopoly has been established,
one can charge large fees for turning raw metal into coins,
and one can debase coins, or use legal compulsion to use these coins.

### Private Mints

Bechtler Mint operated from 1830s through 1850s and produced high-quality coins.

## Fiat Standard

### Bretton-Woods System

In 1944, Bretton-Woods system
allowed Ally countries and their central banks to redeem dollars in gold.
The system has ceased to exist in 1976.

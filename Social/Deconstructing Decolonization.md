# Deconstructing Decolonization

## Decolonization Story

The coming of an empire may bring:

* The end of intertribal Wars and bad customs;
* The science and technology;
* Ideas of Enlightenment.

However, disadvantages are also present.

Note: just as there is no such a single entity as society, there is no one whole minority class.

## Slavery and Emancipation

Slavery is a state in which a person's time and / or property is owned absolutely.
A slave is a person who is a subject of a non-voluntary, a non-contractual relation.

## Empirical Racism

When one makes an argument that one civilization
is biologically inferior to another, it's indeed racism.
However, civilizations do differ technologically, scientifically, and economically.
That is, it's unfair to say that every observation of cultural differences should be attributed
to racism or patronization.

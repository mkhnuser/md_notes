# Cryptography Overview

## Objectives

Objectives of cryptography are:

* Data confidentiality:

      How does one protect its data from an eavesdropper?

* Authentication:

      How does one ensure the the communication party is indeed who they say they are?

* Integrity:

      How does one ensure that the message has not been tempered with?

## Alice, Bob, and Eve

* Alice = a party A;
* Bob = a party B;
* Eve = an eavesdropper.

## Bytes

When you apply cryptographic functions to your data,
always make ensure the data is in bytes,
since errors can arise otherwise.
